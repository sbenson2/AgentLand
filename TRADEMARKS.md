## Artwork

The artwork is copyright the AgentLand project, all rights reserved. That
covers these files:

- the app icon, shared by iPhone, iPad and Mac: `ios/AgentLand/AppIcon.icon/`;
- the PixelLab art (the tent, logo, clowns, characters, circus loader and every
  animation frame):
  - the images in `shared/AgentLandBranding/Sources/AgentLandBranding/Resources/`;
  - the images in `branding/`, including `branding/candidates/`,
    `branding/tent-loop/` and `branding/tightrope/`, and the menu bar tent in
    `branding/tent-menubar.txt`;
- the AgentLand Rubber Hose font,
  `shared/AgentLandBranding/Sources/AgentLandBranding/Resources/Fonts/AgentLandRubberHose.ttf`;
- copies of that art, and screenshots that show it, in
  `docs/prototypes/*/assets/` and `docs/prototypes/*/previews/`.

The code and the JSON data that load and place the art (frame timings, rigs,
geometry) are source code under the MIT license.

Exceptions: the third-party fonts keep their SIL Open Font License, whose text
sits next to each font. They are Alfa Slab One, Bowlby One SC, Bungee, Chango,
Coiny, Holtwood One SC, Shrikhand and Titan One in
`shared/AgentLandBranding/Sources/AgentLandBranding/Resources/Fonts/`;
Diplomata SC, Ewert, Holtwood One SC, Rye and Sancreek in `branding/fonts/`;
and Holtwood One SC in `docs/prototypes/*/assets/`.

## What you may do

- build and run AgentLand from this repository for yourself, under its own name
  and with its artwork;
- say that your project is based on AgentLand, or link to this repository.

If you publish a fork or a build of your own (on an app store, a website or
anywhere else), you must:

- give it a different name that is not confusingly similar to AgentLand;
- replace the app icon, logos, wordmark and all the artwork listed above,
  including the AgentLand Rubber Hose font, with your own;
- change the bundle identifiers, iCloud container and other identifiers listed
  in the README, so your build cannot be mistaken for the official one.

Third-party names and marks (Apple, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Tailscale, Claude,
Codex, OpenCode and others) belong to their owners.
